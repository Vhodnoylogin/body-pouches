VR Interaction Framework: Vhodnoylogin, MIT. Its ordinary-inventory-provider
SDK helper is consumed from pinned public source, not maintained as a copied fork.

HIGGS: FlyingParticle / adamhynek, GPL-3.0. Public API contracts are consumed
from the cooperation-fork revision below. HIGGS itself is a separate dependency;
this archive contains no HIGGS DLL or native HIGGS implementation.
https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/higgs/tree/fd226f6546877fae8b82380ce16aa800000d12ad

CommonLibSSE-NG, fmt, spdlog and rapidcsv are consumed through the separately
recorded RC4 native build and its pinned source inputs. Their license texts are
included in licenses/. Matching source/build references accompany PROVENANCE.

The inventory and worn bag reference Skyrim's original CoinPurseLarge model,
Plants\CoinBagLarge.nif, identified by the owner's Immersive Equipment Displays
(Nexus 62001) visual reference. No IED implementation, mesh or texture is copied.
Skyrim game assets are supplied by the user's installed game and remain Bethesda's.
