## Still required

These remain concept requirements, with further HIGGS/PLANCK goals explicitly deferred by the owner pending author feedback. The current private one-unique-ARMO/native13 demonstration has an offline checked implementation; it is not game qualified or a delivered implementation of the generic requirements below.

- Actual equipment identity/restoration by a consumer, equipment-model binding, content/visual descriptors and preview lifecycle. Logical instances and separate saved per-instance placement are delivered; activation and live handles remain transient.
- Native14 runtime saved-array and full Papyrus/game regression coverage beyond the delivered placement/policy configuration facades. The original global INI semantics must remain distinct from generic save-scoped customization.
- VRIK interaction and presentation for new slots, plus actual tracking validity and model/handedness qualification. Geometry alone cannot draw, deposit or display an item.
- HIGGS input arbitration, exclusive physical resource ownership, coherent possession and correlated transfer outcomes. Temporary restrictions can coexist; they do not arbitrate an exclusive action.
- PLANCK's `DropObject` currently uses legacy `DisableHand` to end an established hold. New admission restrictions preserve that hold; this consumer needs explicit release with a correlated result before changing that call.
- Transfer cancellation across both hands: cancelling a receiver's pending pull cannot undo a donor release queued before a new restriction. A correlated transaction must cover that earlier side effect.
- PLANCK copied actor endpoints, generation-aware attachment requests and bounded hit-policy participation.
- Consumer migrations and Skyrim testing, including real anchors, loading, inventory-to-grip commitment and original14 regression coverage.

## Current internal audit follow-up

The source-backed HIGGS/PLANCK audit remains recorded for later author discussion; further implementation goals are explicitly deferred by the owner.

Current PLANCK already restores pending ragdoll constraints on removal; the earlier premise that restoration was missing is obsolete. Remaining source-backed cases need a larger lifetime contract: tracking can remain active after the animation graph/world disappears, deferred furniture cleanup now rejects work crossing `ResetObjects`, but same-epoch actor/graph changes remain unguarded, and legacy per-actor overrides retain raw actor keys. Counterexamples establish the conditional control-flow gaps, not their frequency or harm in Skyrim. New actor endpoints and queued actions must validate generation and ownership instead of dereferencing saved driver pointers. Constraint-snapshot restoration also performs repeated membership searches; its performance importance needs measurement before optimization.

HIGGS development now repairs the observed-failure history retention found in `FillControllerVelocities`. Invalid callbacks and world/load changes clear the relevant histories; copied availability gates low-speed and directional decisions, and controller-only release contributions become zero without removing player/object motion. A source-level ordering counterexample also prompted monotonic observation tickets: an older valid calculation cannot overwrite a newer invalid observation, even at equal clock times. The separate [velocity ABI](https://github.com/Vhodnoylogin/higgs/blob/development/docs/velocity-observation.md) now exposes source, producer-computed age, space, units and factual history count from that same owner. Consumers still require an explicit age policy. This remains an observation service: hardware tracking, current palm/pose, tracking-to-world continuity, actual dropout behavior and engine scheduling remain unqualified. VRIK's node/finite-position proxy cannot substitute for those facts.
