# Consumer requirements for the shared API/ABI

This review uses twenty different mods to test the expressiveness of the cooperation concept. They are examples, not an allowlist: any Skyrim VR mod should be able to use the shared services within their advertised capabilities. Passing this sample would not establish universal compatibility.

This is a requirements review, not a claim that the APIs or consumer migrations are complete. The [English concept](../trinity-synergy/concept-en.md), [Russian concept](../trinity-synergy/concept-ru.md) and [implementation ledger](../trinity-synergy/implementation-status.md) retain their separate purposes. Provider source, actual consumer ports, native builds and game acceptance must be reported independently.

## Evidence and versions

Read-only review on 2 November 2026: local archive members, configuration files, readmes and supplied Papyrus source, plus primary public C++ source where available. No closed DLL was decompiled or treated as understood from its strings. No installation or game test was performed. A setting establishes a consumer requirement, not its native implementation. Source not inspected does not mean no source exists anywhere.

Versions identify the local inputs examined, not a promise of the latest upstream release. Public source snapshots are pinned in the [compact requirements and provenance dataset](consumer-contract-requirements.json), together with archive hashes. Source HEADs are not assumed binary-identical to archives. For example, the local lockpicking archive is 1.0.1, while Nexus already lists 1.1.0. No third-party assets or source are copied into this repository.

## Implementation-aware criticism

The [Russian per-mod assessment](consumer-api-review-ru.md) explains the concrete simplification and remaining blocker for each of the twenty consumers. The matrix above records the inspected requirements; it must not be read as a declaration that every requested service is missing or that any original consumer has migrated.

Already delivered primitives include extended controller controls/hold/multitap (Archery), contact/activation-started hand sessions (Torches and Lockpicking), a scoped ignore-global-ForcePhysicsGrab preference (Smithing), articulated hand boxes plus existing NPC compound-capsule/drive proposals (PPB), and equipment-supplied persistent Body Slots plus the ordinary-inventory provider helper (Body Pouches). The helper is protocol SDK code, not an installed pouch or a generic executor for every item domain.

The strongest counterexamples are composition and missing physical/domain authority:

- Harvesting can catch an insect with one hand, add a second pinch and release only that pinch. A request/renew/end API without atomic footprint changes forces it to abandon existing authority or reserve an unused hand. A revision-checked transition must retain the original grant and refuse removal until dependent native work drains. The separate HandUseProposals table now collects nonbutton opportunities into a configured user-priority round. It preserves existing uses and atomically accepts disjoint hand masks; real harvesting/climbing ports remain outstanding.
- Player-inventory transfers do not implement NPC theft, worn biped swaps, torch transitions, ammo equipment, crafting or projectile recall/extraction. These need typed domain executors and exact observed results, not a broader claim on an ordinary item ticket.
- Reserving a hand cannot replace PPB pose-stop, TrueWield motor/gravity/impedance or Lockpicking tool goals. Supported physical targets, applied state, force/saturation evidence and rendered-hand readiness are distinct capabilities.
- PPB's old form/child contact followed by a current geometry query cannot survive intervening model replacement safely. Anatomy must correlate generations and applied geometry revision, disclose coverage and support removable drained subscriptions.
- Full trigger masking cannot substitute for Stop Trigger's narrow weapon-draw policy. Fresh UI controls, pre-boundary held-button drainage, post-UI drainage and gameplay action admission require one coherent context boundary. CustomMenu detection alone does not provide it.
- Own weapon/shield contact requires exact bilateral ownership and relative velocity at one contact point, including angular motion. Audio/haptic admission is independent of solver collision and hit/damage policy. A copied touch notification is not proof that a clang reached the device.

These findings guide the next ABI changes. Published source, consumer ports, native builds and live acceptance remain separately recorded in the [implementation ledger](../trinity-synergy/implementation-status.md).

## Requirements shared across consumers

- **A hand use can begin without a controller press.** A lock activation, posture, contact or dwell can start a session. The shared hand-state and operation vocabulary remains finite: lockpicking, skinning and smithing are consumer contexts and rules, rather than another hand-state enum for each mod. Sessions need acquisition, a hand footprint, renewal, cancellation, drainage and actual readiness before dependent gameplay starts.
- **Input is broader than grip and trigger.** These consumers use other buttons, axes, touch, holds and multi-taps. Observations alone do not resolve overlapping gestures or ownership of the final game action. A provider must declare what it can arbitrate, and consumed inputs must remain masked until release when a session ends.
- **Item identity crosses several domains.** Inventory entries, equipped items, HIGGS-held world references, proxies, projectiles and slot assignments are not interchangeable base forms. Transfers need exact identities, preserved instance properties, correlated stages and an explicit partial-failure outcome. Crafting, crime, recall, ammo and assignment policy remain with domain executors.
- **Physical requests need observations as well as controls.** Mass, inertia, centre of mass, grip geometry, each hand's grip relation, motion frames and actual constraint/drive outcomes matter to weight, throwing and extraction. Acceptance is not proof of an effective physical operation.
- **Temporary changes must compose.** Grab modes, hand presentation, collision restrictions, haptics and physical goals need declared owners and lifetimes. Saving a global value and restoring it later can overwrite another mod's request. HIGGS remains the final hand/weapon actuator; PLANCK remains the NPC physical-body lifetime owner.
- **Not every spatial region is a body slot.** Body Slots owns player body/equipment slots and their interaction. PPB supplies anatomy; workbench, fire and lock providers retain their domain geometry unless an explicitly supported shared spatial primitive is appropriate. CommonLib can provide technical geometry/engine helpers without taking over gameplay rules.
- **Existing Papyrus consumers need a practical route.** A native ABI can serve a new native mod, while an existing PSC consumer needs a Papyrus event/request adapter or a deliberate consumer port. This is a delivery choice to record, not evidence that the original scripts already consume the ABI.
- **A dependency must earn its place.** A simple perk toggle or animation-speed patch may gain nothing directly from a slot/physics service. Optional integration and an explicit no-gain conclusion are valid outcomes.

## Assessing another consumer

Describe its hand resources, input or non-input start, observations, requested physical/equipment operations and domain executor. Map each to a negotiated capability with a lifetime, phase, coordinate space and terminal outcome. Exercise the combination against a competing consumer, replacement/load invalidation and partial failure; independent successful demonstrations do not prove composition.

If a required shared operation is absent, record the gap and extend the responsible provider's versioned contract. If it is specific gameplay, keep it in a domain executor. A new consumer does not justify another hidden hand driver, a magic unknown-state detector or a growing list of consumer-specific hand states. Report implementation and qualification in the [implementation ledger](../trinity-synergy/implementation-status.md), rather than infer compatibility from this matrix.
